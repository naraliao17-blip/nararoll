This is an example of a text-only post. It has no `cover` field in its frontmatter, so it appears in **Writing** but not in the **Photography** grid.

A post can also mix the two — add a `cover` and drop more `![alt](/photos/...)` images anywhere in the body, in between paragraphs, and it will show up in both places at once.
